Boiled Egg



Description

Perfect boiled eggs depend entirely on precision timing and an immediate ice bath. Lowering cold eggs directly into boiling water shocks the egg whites, making peeling effortless. Adjusting the timer by just a minute or two easily customizes the yolk from a liquid center to a dense, creamy texture.



Ingredients
  • large egss
  • 1 table spoon salt
  • water
  • ice cubes


Steps
  1. Boil the water: Fill a pot with enough water to fully cover the eggs and bring it to a rolling boil.

  2. Add the eggs: Lower cold eggs gently into the boiling water using a spoon to prevent cracking.

  3. Simmer and time: Lower the heat slightly to a gentle boil and set a timer for 6 minutes (soft-boiled), 8 minutes (jammy), or 10 to 12 minutes (hard-boiled).

  4. Chill in ice: Immediately transfer the cooked eggs into a bowl filled with ice water for 3 to 5 minutes.

  5. Peel and serve: Tap the egg gently on a hard surface to crack the shell, then peel starting from the wider bottom end.
