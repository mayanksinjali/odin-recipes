Pancakes



Description

Classic pancakes are a beloved breakfast staple celebrated for their light, fluffy texture and golden-brown exterior. The secret to their fluffiness lies in a gentle hand, as leaving small lumps in the batter ensures the gluten does not overdevelop and make them tough. Cooked on a hot griddle, they rise beautifully and create the perfect canvas for your favorite toppings.



Ingredients
  • All-purpose flour
  • Baking powder and salt
  • Granulated sugar
  • Whole milk
  • Large egg
  • Melted unsalted butter
